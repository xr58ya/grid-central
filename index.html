Coming soon · For iRacing on Windows

Your race engineer,
in your ear.

Grid Central calls the cars around you, coaches you corner by corner, works out your fuel and pit stops, and answers when you ask. It runs on your PC alongside iRacing.

It's being finished with a small group of beta testers now, and opens to everyone soon.

What it's for

Everything a race engineer does, on one screen.

Live data comes straight from iRacing on your PC. Nothing about your driving leaves your computer unless you share it.

SPOTTER

Voice spotter and engineer

Car left, car right, clear. Flags, gaps, fuel, damage, weather and pit calls, read out in a natural voice and never talking over each other.

TALKBACK

Ask, and it answers

Hold a wheel button and ask "how's the fuel?" or "gap behind?", or tell it "four new tyres, fill it up". Recognised offline, in English, Spanish or German.

COACH

Find your next tenth

Every lap is compared with your best, corner by corner: braking, apex speed, exits. One focus corner at a time until it's fixed.

STRATEGY

Fuel to the finish

Live fuel per lap, laps to go, the pit window and what to add. It can even set the fuel in iRacing's pit menu for you.

SETUPS

Setup help that makes sense

Say "it's understeering" or "it bottoms out" and get the garage change to try. Compare setups by the laps you drove on them.

OVERLAYS

Overlays for stream and screen

Design your own from widgets, or start from a template: standings, relative, fuel, tyres, flags, track map and more. Add it to OBS in one click, or show it over iRacing while you drive.

BROADCAST

Race commentary for your stream

Two commentators call the race like TV: overtakes, battles, pit stops, flags and the finish. Your viewers hear them through OBS or any streaming app; you don't.

PAINT SHOP

Paint your car, helmet and suit

Start from iRacing's template and patterns, recolour, add numbers and logos, choose a finish from matte to chrome, and watch it change on your car in iRacing as you paint.

TEAM

Phone pit wall and team hub

Live timing and strategy on your phone, and a team page your crew can follow from anywhere. Driver swaps and team messages are read out to the driver.

DISCORD

Results in your league's Discord

Race results and session summaries with fuel use and tyre wear, personal bests, "going live" posts with your stream links, and a league bot for schedules and sign-ups.

Who it's for

For drivers, streamers and teams.

Grid Central speaks English, Spanish and German.

Drivers

From your first race to an endurance stint: a spotter that never misses a car, a coach that finds your next tenth, and strategy that gets you to the flag.

Streamers

Overlays you design yourself, two commentators your viewers hear and you don't, and OBS control from Grid Central or your phone.

Teams and leagues

A phone pit wall and a team page your crew can follow from anywhere, driver swaps read out in the car, and results posted to your league's Discord.
