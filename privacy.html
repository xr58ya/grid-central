Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

  • Your laps, setups, paints and settings stay on your PC.
  • Your account holds your email, your plan and which PC your subscription is on.
  • What you choose to share, such as the team hub or Discord posts, goes where you send it.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, fix or delete your data.

1. Who we are

Grid Central ("we" or "us") is based in New South Wales, Australia. We're responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at support@gridcentral.store.

2. What stays on your PC

  • Laps, telemetry, setups, paints, overlays, debriefs, settings and the phrases you teach Talkback are kept on your PC, along with Grid Central's log file (companion.log).
  • The phone pit wall connects your phone to your PC over your own network, not through our servers.
  • Talkback listens only while you hold your talk button, and turns your speech into text on your PC. No audio is saved or sent.
  • The stream commentary's natural voices are made on your PC.

4. Signing in with iRacing

If you sign in with iRacing, you do it on iRacing's own page, and we never see your iRacing password. The sign-in is kept only on your PC, encrypted to your Windows account. Grid Central uses it to fetch your results and ratings, which stay on your PC. Signing out cancels the sign-in at iRacing too.

5. Voices

  • Windows' own voices are made on your PC.
  • If you choose one of Microsoft Edge's natural voices (marked ★ natural), Edge sends the text of each spoken call to Microsoft's online speech service to turn it into sound. Microsoft's privacy statement covers that. To keep every call on your PC, choose a Windows voice.

6. Discord and OBS

  • Rich Presence shows what you're doing in Grid Central on your Discord profile, through the Discord app on your PC. You can turn it off.
  • Webhook posts and the league bot post the results, messages and sign-ups you set up to your Discord server. Your webhook link and bot token stay on your PC.
  • OBS control works between Grid Central and OBS on your PC or your own network.

7. Downloads and online content

When Grid Central downloads something, the server it downloads from sees your IP address and basic details about the request, as any website does. No account details are sent, and we don't receive this information.

  • GitHub: updates, the beta code list, the lap library, the commentary voices and Talkback's speech models. GitHub also hosts the team page that Grid Central links to.
  • iRacing: track map shapes.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts in Grid Central, its overlay and this website.
  • jsDelivr: a library the paint shop loads to open Photoshop (.psd) files.

9. Where your data is kept

Our providers store and handle data in the United States and other countries. When your data leaves your country, we rely on our providers' safeguards for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account, plan and team data: while you have an account. Delete my account in Grid Central (Settings → Account) deletes it straight away, with a team you own; if you ask us instead, we delete it within 30 days.
  • The free trial record and beta codes: after an account is deleted, we keep the PC's random ID, its hardware fingerprints, the internet address fingerprint, the email address fingerprint and the fingerprint of a beta code it used (without your account or email), so the free trial stays once per PC and once per email address, and each beta code is used once. Download my data includes these fingerprints while you have an account.
  • Moves to another PC: while you have an account; deleting your account deletes them.
  • Counts of requests: 2 days.
  • Purchase records, Lemon Squeezy's messages about them included: as long as tax and accounting law requires (in Australia, generally 5 years).
  • Team hub: your car's data is replaced every 2 seconds while you drive. Team chat stays in the team's room until you ask us to delete it. Deleting your account removes your car from your team's room, and the room itself if the team is yours.
  • Help desk tickets and support emails: up to 1 year after the conversation ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Resend keeps its own record of the emails it sent for a limited time.

11. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, along with your account;
  • stop or limit some uses of it.

In Grid Central, Download my data (Settings → Account) saves a copy of what our server holds about your account, and Delete my account deletes it and cancels your subscriptions. You can also email support@gridcentral.store from the address on your account, and we'll reply within 30 days.

To delete a help desk ticket, email support from the address you used, with its ticket number (for example GC-1001). We'll delete the ticket, its files and history, and the emails about it in our inbox.

If you're in the EU, EEA or UK, we use your information to provide the service you asked for, to keep Grid Central secure and the free trial fair (our legitimate interests), and to meet legal duties. Optional features, such as the team hub and Discord, run only when you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. In Australia, you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. In the EU, EEA or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security

Everything Grid Central sends goes over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database rules let each account read only its own records, your iRacing sign-in is encrypted on your PC, and the payments server's keys are kept secret. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

13. Children

Grid Central is for adults: you must be 18 or older to use it. If you think someone under 18 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For a significant change, we'll also tell you in the app or by email.
